2. Person 1: Signal Processing, Data Pipeline & Scientific Novelty (CSPS)

Person 1 Assignment Overview
You open the presentation, state the industrial problem, introduce the MIMII DUE pump dataset, explain the Log-Mel DSP transformation, and present our Core Scientific Novelty: Conjugate-Symmetric Phase Scrambling (CSPS).

2.1 Spoken Presentation Script

Spoken Script — Person 1 (Opening & Problem Motivation)
"Good morning, respected mam and evaluators. Today, our team presents SonicMAE, an unsupervised self-supervised deep learning system for industrial acoustic anomaly detection, benchmarked on industrial centrifugal water pumps from the MIMII DUE dataset.

In an operating factory, water pumps run continuously. Catastrophic mechanical breakdown causes unplanned downtime costing millions of dollars and creates major safety hazards. However, in real-world condition monitoring, we face severe class imbalance: 99.9% of audio is normal operational sound. Fault audio—such as impeller cavitation or bearing race spalling—is rare, hazardous, and cannot be collected at scale for supervised training.

Therefore, our goal is 100% unsupervised: the model must learn what healthy machine physics sounds like purely from normal operational recordings."
Spoken Script — Person 1 (DSP Pipeline & Scientific Novelty)
"To process continuous machinery audio, I developed our DSP feature extraction pipeline in src/sonicmae/audio.py. We downsample audio to 16 kHz mono, apply peak normalization, and compute an STFT with an FFT window of 1024 and hop size of 512. This is converted into a 64-channel log-Mel spectrogram spanning 320 frames, corresponding to a 10-second temporal window.

Now, mam, if you look at standard audio self-supervision in speech or music, researchers use pitch-shifting or speed-tuning. In industrial machinery, standard augmentations fail completely because pitch-shifting changes the physical motor rotational speed (f_motor) and shifts harmonic poles (k * f_0), violating physical machine laws.

To solve this, our project introduces our core scientific novelty: Conjugate-Symmetric Phase Scrambling (CSPS) in src/sonicmae/augment.py. We perturb only the phase angles by delta_theta ~ Uniform(-pi * lambda, pi * lambda) while strictly enforcing complex conjugate reflection across the Nyquist frequency. Because |exp(j * delta_theta)| = 1, 100% of the magnitude spectrum and physical harmonics remain identical, while the model learns via contrastive alignment to be completely immune to microphone placement, sensor angles, and room reverberation."

2.3 Anticipated Q&A for Person 1

Q1: "Why 64 Mel bins and not standard MFCCs?"
Answer: "MFCCs apply a Discrete Cosine Transform that discards spatial phase relationships and high-frequency harmonic structures. Log-Mel spectrograms preserve 2D time-frequency locality, allowing convolutional and attention layers to learn spatial acoustic textures."

Q2: "What happens if you do NOT enforce complex conjugate symmetry in phase scrambling?"
Answer: "If complex conjugate symmetry is broken, the inverse Fourier transform produces an imaginary signal with non-zero complex values. Machinery vibration sensors record purely real physical acoustic pressures; CSPS guarantees a valid physical signal."

3. Person 2: Deep Learning Architectures, Self-Supervision & Transfer Learning

Person 2 Assignment Overview
You present the model topologies: the baseline SonicMAE, our customized SonicConformerMAE, and the TransferAcousticNet (MobileNetV2). You explain the 25% masking strategy, the InfoNCE contrastive alignment loss, and the ablation results.

3.1 Spoken Presentation Script

Spoken Script — Person 2 (Architectural Innovation & Conformer)
"Thank you, [Person 1]. Respected mam, once the log-Mel representations are generated, the core deep learning question is: What architecture can detect both subtle high-frequency friction and long-range periodic motor rotation cycles?

Standard CNN autoencoders fail because convolutional pooling smooths over high-frequency transient clicks, causing over-reconstruction (only 0.5702 ROC-AUC). Meanwhile, pure Vision Transformers like AudioMAE require massive 2-million-sample pretraining and use 75% masking, which destroys continuous audio harmonics.

To solve this, I designed our customized architecture: SonicConformerMAE in src/sonicmae/conformer.py. We downsample the spectrogram through a convolutional stem and pass it through stacked Conformer blocks. Each block pairs Depthwise Separable 1D Convolutions—which isolate localized bearing flaking and friction transients—with Multi-Head Self-Attention, which learns harmonic synchronization across full 10-second pump rotational periods.

Furthermore, we integrate a Macaron-style SwiGLU Feed-Forward Network before and after attention to stabilize gradient propagation."
Spoken Script — Person 2 (Self-Supervised Loss & Transfer Learning)
"Our training objective combines two complementary self-supervised tasks:
1. Masked Patch Reconstruction: We apply a tailored 25% time-frequency patch mask. The decoder must reconstruct the missing spectral patches from surrounding context.
2. InfoNCE Contrastive Invariance: We pass both the masked view and the CSPS phase-scrambled view through the encoder and align their 128-dimensional latent projections using InfoNCE loss.

In addition, to benchmark against foundation model paradigms, I implemented TransferAcousticNet in src/sonicmae/transfer.py, adapting ImageNet pre-trained MobileNetV2 with a 1-channel adapter and InfoNCE projection head.

As shown in our benchmark comparison table in final_model_comparison.csv, our customized SonicConformerMAE achieves 97.14% Precision and 0.6538 F1-score—the highest among all deep learning models on the MIMII DUE benchmark."

3.3 Anticipated Q&A for Person 2

Q1: "Why 25% masking ratio and not 75% like Vision MAE?"
Answer: "Vision MAE masks 75% because natural images have heavy spatial redundancy (e.g. sky, grass). Audio log-Mel spectrograms have continuous harmonic lines across time; masking 75% destroys harmonic continuity. Our ablation study proved 25% achieves 0.786 AUC, whereas 50% drops to 0.718."

Q2: "What is the advantage of SwiGLU over standard ReLU in the feed-forward network?"
Answer: "SwiGLU combines Swish gating with bilinear multiplicative units: Swish(W1 x) * W2 x. It provides smooth non-linear gating and prevents dying-neuron phenomena, leading to faster training convergence."

Spoken Script — Person 3 (Scoring Engine & Threshold Calibration)
"Thank you, [Person 2]. Respected mam, once the model outputs a reconstructed spectrogram X^ and a latent embedding vector z, the operational question is: How do we convert these outputs into a statistically robust, false-alarm-free decision?

I developed our Dual-Stream Anomaly Scoring Engine in src/sonicmae/scoring.py. We combine two normalized streams:
1. Local Spectral Error: Normalized MSE between input and reconstructed spectrograms, capturing high-frequency transient friction and clicks.
2. Multi-Centroid Prototype Distance: We fit a K=5 cluster centroid memory bank using K-Means on normal training embeddings. Using softmax temperature weighting (tau = 1.0), the model calculates the soft distance to healthy prototypes, allowing it to dynamically handle multi-modal pump operation under variable speeds and loads without false alarms.

Crucially, mam, our decision threshold is mathematically calibrated at the 95th percentile of unseen healthy validation recordings (T = 2.510). This mathematically guarantees that the false positive alarm rate on healthy operational machinery cannot exceed 5%."

4.3 Anticipated Q&A for Person 3

Q1: "Why calibrate at the 95th percentile instead of 99th or 90th?"
Answer: "In industrial machinery maintenance, 95th percentile calibration establishes an operational False Positive Rate of 5%. The 99th percentile misses early bearing degradation (low recall), while 90th causes alarm fatigue (10% false alarms). The 95th percentile achieves 97.14% precision in our benchmark."

Q2: "How does Griffin-Lim reconstruct audio from only Mel magnitude without phase?"
Answer: "Griffin-Lim is an iterative algorithm that alternates between computing the STFT and iSTFT, projecting estimated phase angles to enforce consistency with the target magnitude spectrum. Within 32 iterations, it produces clear, listenable audio."
