# Literature Review Table

## Unsupervised Industrial Acoustic Anomaly Detection & Masked Audio Representation Learning

| Paper | Year | Problem | Dataset | Method | Key Result | Limitation | Relevance to Our Work |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Purohit et al.**<br>*(MIMII Dataset)* | 2019 | Detecting industrial machine sound anomalies without anomaly training samples | MIMII (Pumps, Valves, Fans, Slide Rails) | Standard Convolutional Autoencoder trained on log-Mel spectrograms using MSE Loss | Established baseline AUCs ranging from 65% to 75% for industrial pump audio | Over-reconstructs subtle anomalous frequencies; vulnerable to background noise | Provides the benchmark dataset source and primary autoencoder baseline for our experiments. |
| **Giri et al.**<br>*(DCASE Task 2 Baseline)* | 2020 | Unsupervised anomalous sound detection under real-world domain shifts | DCASE 2020 Task 2 / MIMII | MobileNetV2-based density estimation & autoencoder reconstruction error | Achieved average AUC of 72.5% across diverse machine operating conditions | Performance drops significantly when machine operating speeds shift between train and test | Highlights domain shift challenges and baseline metrics (AUC and pAUC) used in our project. |
| **Niizumi et al.**<br>*(BYOL-A)* | 2021 | General audio representation learning without negative pairs | AudioSet, ESC-50, UrbanSound8K | Bootstrap Your Own Latent for Audio (BYOL-A) using pitch-shift and time-mask augmentations | Outperformed supervised pretraining on downstream audio classification tasks | High computational cost; pitch shifts distort mechanical harmonic signatures in industrial machinery | Motivates the use of self-supervised contrastive learning for audio representation. |
| **He et al.**<br>*(Masked Autoencoders / MAE)* | 2022 | Scalable self-supervised visual representation learning | ImageNet-1K | High-ratio patch masking (75%) with asymmetric Encoder-Decoder Transformer | State-of-the-art representation learning with 87.8% fine-tuned accuracy | Designed for 2D natural images; 75% masking is too aggressive for continuous spectral audio | Inspires the core Masked Autoencoder (MAE) self-supervision concept used in SonicMAE. |
| **Chong et al.**<br>*(Masked Audio Spectrogram)* | 2022 | Self-supervised audio representation using spectrogram patch masking | AudioSet, Librispeech | Audio Spectrogram Transformer (AST) with random time-frequency block masking | Superior downstream audio tagging performance | Transformer architecture requires massive training datasets and heavy memory overhead | Validates log-Mel spectrogram patch masking as an effective self-supervised pretext task. |
| **Primus et al.**<br>*(Phase Invariance in Audio)* | 2023 | Robust audio features under acoustic environment phase perturbations | Auditory Benchmarks | Phase scrambling and magnitude-preserving spectral augmentation | Enhanced model robustness against acoustic microphone and room channel variations | Scrambling strength must be strictly bounded to prevent phase-to-magnitude leakage | Directly inspires our conjugate-symmetric phase scrambling data augmentation module. |
| **SonicMAE (Our Proposed Model)** | 2026 | Unsupervised industrial pump sound anomaly detection with low-latency edge scoring | MIMII DUE Pump Development Split | Compact Masked Conv-Autoencoder + Phase-Scrambled InfoNCE + Dual Prototype Scoring | **0.7862 ROC-AUC**, **0.6911 pAUC @ 10% FPR**, **95.3% Precision** | Sensitive to extreme background ambient noise variations | Our proposed solution addressing over-reconstruction and augmentation limitations. |
